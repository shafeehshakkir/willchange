How to drive

1. Load a song

Click Load MP3 and pick a file. Click once on the page so the browser allows sound.

2. Start the engine

Mash A (or Space / the Crank button) about 5 times quickly until the engine catches.

3. Pedals

  • Clutch — bar matches how hard you press
  • Throttle — same idea, variable pressure

4. Shift the H-gate

  1. Press the clutch in past about 70%
  2. Flick the right stick toward a gear — the knob completes the H path and snaps into that gate
  3. Release the stick anytime; it finishes the move. No mid-rail parking and no diagonal teleports
  4. Ease the clutch out while adding throttle so you don’t stall

5. Drive the music

Every gear plays the song — 1st is a clear half-speed cruise. Higher gears unlock faster playback toward normal speed. Push too hard in a low gear and you hit redline (shift up for more song speed). Clutch in to free-rev; dump it at low RPM and you’ll stall.

Mistakes

  • Grind — changing gear with clutch below ~70% (change rejected)
  • Stall — RPM falls below ~400 while in gear with the clutch out
  • If stalled, mash A again to restart

Keyboard

  • Q clutch · E throttle
  • 1–5 gears · 6 reverse · 0/N neutral
  • Space ignition · ? toggle this guide

Bindings

Use the bindings panel under the dash if your controller mapping feels wrong.
